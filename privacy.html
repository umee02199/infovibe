Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data InfoVibe handles.

1. Data We Collect

On this demo build, no personal data is sent to any server. Account details, comments, and bookings are stored locally in your own browser via localStorage.

2. Third-Party Services

  • Google Translate — provides the language widget. Google may process page text you choose to translate.
  • WhatsApp — clicking the chat button opens WhatsApp with a prefilled message; no data is shared until you send it.
  • CDN (Three.js, fonts) — loaded from public CDNs for the 3D hero and typography.

3. Cookies

We use only essential local storage (theme preference, session, demo data). No advertising or tracking cookies are set.

4. Your Rights

You can clear all local data anytime by clearing your browser storage for this site.
